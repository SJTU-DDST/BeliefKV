# Primary Sources Bound for the V75 Research Screening

Downloaded on 2026-10-16. `SHA256SUMS` binds the original arXiv abstract pages,
PDFs and `pdftotext -layout` outputs. These are research inputs, not executed
baselines or evidence of conference acceptance.
The directory attributes preserve original PDF bytes and exclude untouched
third-party HTML whitespace from source-code whitespace checks.

- TokenDance, arXiv:2604.03143v1, sections 2.2, 4.1-4.4 and 6.6.
  Source: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03143`.
  Its collective path groups compatible All-Gather requests; its prototype
  uses CacheBlend selective recomputation and Master-Mirror storage. The
  equivalence in section 6.6 is to that PIC path, not exact original-model
  inference. The paper does not establish correctness or performance for our
  Qwen3.5 FULL/Mamba path.
- SAGA, arXiv:2605.00528v2, sections 3-6.
  Source: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.00528v2`.
  Agent Execution Graphs, probabilistic retention, session-affinity batching
  and agent-level fairness constrain any claim based on generic workflow
  dependencies or scheduling.

the complete V75 predictive arm. That audit does not establish equal rendered
